A fantasy game that respects your football brain.

Traditional fantasy flattens football into four positions and two questions: did he score, and did they keep a clean sheet? EliteScout gives you real positions, a transfer budget, a live market where prices move with form, and 24 stats that reward watching every minute.

Dynamic Prices

Prices move like a real market.

Every player's price recalculates each gameweek based on their rolling form. A hot streak means their price climbs. A dip means bargains. Your squad value grows if you scout smart — and shrinks if you sit still.

  • 55 trades per season, 2 per gameweek
  • 7 boost tokens to double down on your best hunches
  • £500m transfer budget — every signing has to earn its place
Live Scoring

Every kick counts, in real time.

Goals, assists, shots on target, key passes, tackles, blocks, clean sheets — all scored live as the match unfolds. Your gameweek total updates minute-by-minute, not once the whistle blows.

  • Auto emergency subs when your starter doesn't play
  • Match-up view: your XI vs your rival's, side by side, live
